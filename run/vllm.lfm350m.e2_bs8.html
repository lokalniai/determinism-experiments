Sunset times are often red or orange because the sun is lower in the sky, and the scattering of sunlight by the atmosphere is more pronounced during this time. Dust particles can scatter sunlight in a way that makes the sky appear darker or more muted, while pollution can introduce additional colored particles that alter the sky's appearance.
